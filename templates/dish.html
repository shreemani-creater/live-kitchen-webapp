VEG ROLL

INGREDIANTS REQUIRED

* 1/2 cup maida flour
* 1/2 cup wheat flour
* Salt
* Tomato 2nos
* Onion 2nos
* Carrot 1nos
* Capsicum 1nos
* Cumin

* Garam powder
* Pepper powder
* Chili powder
* Tomato sauce
* Chili sauce
* Oil
* Butter / Cheese (optional)

STEP-1 :Prepare & Soak for Roti

Add maida & wheat flour,Add required salt and water,Mix well

Knead(pisaiyavum) well,Soak for 20min

STEP-2 :Cutting Vegitables

Cutting Vegitables into your favourite sizes

STEP-3 :Start Cooking

Heat pan,Add oil,Add cummin(1-table spoon),Stewing some time

Add Onion, Stewing until Onion get light brown color

Add Tomato,Capsicum,carrot, Stewing well all Vegitable,
Add required salt

Inbetween stewing vegitables
Add garam(2-table spoon),chilli(1/2-table spoon)mix well
Your stuff will be ready,Take out the pan.

STEP-4 :Make Roti for Roll(Big size)

Make roti in big size,it will gives to make a roll comfortable

Bake roti both sides in medium level,it will be soft
roti is ready.

STEP-5 :Ready To Make Roti Roll

Apply tomato,chili sauce in full roti

Add prepared vegi stuff in center of the roti
Finally add seasoning pepper and optional (cheese)
gives you better taste with aroma.

make a roll in the given image

Close both sides and punch using toothpic stick

Veg roll is ready to serve
